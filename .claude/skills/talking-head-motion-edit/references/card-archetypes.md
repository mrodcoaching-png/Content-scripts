# Framing modes and card archetypes

## Four framing modes

Pick one per sentence based on how much visual weight the graphic needs versus the speaker.

| Mode | Layout | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `split` | Video in a bottom band, a 1:1 graphic square on top | The workhorse — most sentences |
| `solo` | Speaker centered, graphic banners above and below | Simpler lines, transitions between bigger beats |
| `fullhim` | Full-bleed tight crop on the speaker, floating chips/labels over the footage | Reactive or emphatic lines that still need the face |
| `full` | Video hidden entirely, full-screen graphic takeover | Big single-idea beats only — cap these at roughly 3 per video, they lose impact if overused |

Hard-cut into and out of `full`; tween smoothly between `split` and `solo` since both keep
the video visible. In `split`, content lives in the top square and captions own the bottom
strip — don't let them compete for the same space.

## Beat → card archetype

Match what's actually being said to a proven design rather than inventing a new layout per
line — consistency across the edit reads as more intentional than novelty per sentence.

| The beat in the script | The card |
|---|---|
| A number is spoken | Giant count-up animating to the number, sub-label appears after it lands |
| "The status quo is wrong" / a common mistake is named | A real screenshot in a white card, with a rotated STAMP graphic landing on the key word |
| "From this, to this" — a before/after claim | Two real screenshots slide in on their respective words: before-state styled as a negative, after-state as a positive, an arrow between them |
| "Everyone says X, Y, Z, but..." — a list being dismissed | Each item pops in as a row, gets struck through in sequence, then the real answer appears, visually distinct (glowing/highlighted) |
| An emotional one-word beat | Full-screen takeover: one giant word, nothing else |
| Social proof (testimonials, ratings, review counts) | A count-up plus star/rating icons, real reviews as background/B-roll if available |
| A guarantee or promise | An outlined shield or badge shape, one pulse animation, no more |
| CTA (final ~3 seconds) | Logo, one offer line, a pulsing button, a bounce-arrow pointing at it |

Rules worth holding to across the whole edit: every number that's spoken gets a count-up, no
exceptions — it's cheap to build once and pays off everywhere. Every list pops in
word-synced with the line being spoken, not all at once.
