# Default style: "explainer reel" (vertical 9:16)

Reverse-engineered from the source reel (Colton Dean, "Claude can fully edit
videos now"). Use it when the user has no style of their own.

## Layout
- Speaker fills the frame; all motion graphics live in the **top ~45%**, over
  the background wall, never over the face.
- **Section label** top-left, white, medium weight, ~5% of frame height:
  "HyperFrames", "The easy setup", "01 Install it", "03 Explain the edit".
  Number the steps ("01", "02", "03") when the script walks through a process.
- **Captions** sit just above the speaker's head (about 45-50% down): 2-4 words
  at a time, white, bold, soft shadow, synced word-by-word to the transcript.
- **Emphasis captions**: for a punchline ("Not editing anything."), show the
  full phrase larger near the bottom third with an underline that draws in.

## Card vocabulary (what to put on screen)
- **Title card** (first 2-3s): big condensed all-caps headline of the promise,
  e.g. "HOW TO HAVE CLAUDE EDIT VIDEOS".
- **Tool card**: screenshot/mockup of the repo or product, tilted slightly, plus
  pill tags ("OPEN SOURCE", "APACHE-2.0") and a big word ("FREE").
- **Concept visual**: an animated audio waveform with gaps labeled "DEAD" to show
  dead space being cut; a big "0" with "EDITS BY ME"; a timer "1:01 RAW";
  crossed-out boxes labeled "RETAKE"/"MISTAKE".
- **Prompt mockup**: a frosted-glass chat box with a "Claude" chip and a send
  button, typing out the instruction being described ("Install this:
  github.com/...", "Put animations above me showing what I'm talking about").
  A progress bar finishing with "INSTALLED" works for setup steps.

## Look & motion
- Frosted/translucent dark-gray cards (backdrop blur, 16-20px radius), white
  text, monospace for small tags/URLs, no loud brand colors.
- Entrances: 0.25-0.4s fade + 20-40px slide/scale-up with ease-out; text
  typing for prompts; numbers count up.
- New visual every ~2-4s, swapped at sentence boundaries. Exit quickly
  (0.15-0.2s) so the next beat lands on its word.
