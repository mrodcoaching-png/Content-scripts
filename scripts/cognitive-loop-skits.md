# Cognitive loop skits: consistency

- Format modeled on: reels/matt-bitter-2026-10-11 (Matt Bitter, "The cognitive loop can dictate your reality")
- Skill: cognitive-loop-skit
- Topic: behavioral consistency / taking action
- Length target: ~15-20s each, under 60 words, one talking-head shot plus one time-jump cut
- Film all three on the same day so you have a series ready

## Filming notes (all three)

- Mid-shot, looking slightly off camera, as if you're thinking out loud. Say it calmly, like it all makes sense to you. Don't play it for laughs.
- Lines 1 to 5 are one take. Make a hard cut before line 6.
- After the cut, use the **same outfit, same spot and same angle**. Nothing changing is the punchline. Say line 6 flatly, with no expression.
- Optional on-screen text over the cut: "5 YEARS LATER".
- End right on the last word so the video loops back to line 1.

---

## 1. The Monday loop (strongest, so post this one first)

**Belief in the loop:** "I'll start properly on Monday."

**Script** (~15s)
1. I'll start properly on Monday.
2. There's no point starting halfway through the week.
3. Had a pizza on Wednesday. Well, that's this week gone.
4. I'll start properly on Monday.
5. Better to start fresh than drag a bad week into it.
6. Two hundred and sixty Mondays later. Still starting.

**On-screen text:** "260 MONDAYS LATER" on the cut

**Prop idea:** a wall calendar behind you with every Monday circled

**Caption**
Monday isn't a fresh start. It's a way of putting it off that sounds like a plan.
The smallest version you can do today beats the perfect version you'll start next week.
Comment TODAY and I'll send you the 5-minute starter plan.

## 2. The "not a consistent person" loop

**Belief in the loop:** "I'm just not a consistent person."

**Script** (~18s)
1. I don't know… I'm just not a consistent person.
2. Some people are disciplined. I'm more all-or-nothing.
3. I did really well for two weeks. Then I missed a few days. Classic me.
4. I'm just not a consistent person.
5. I'll go again when I'm properly motivated. No point doing it half-hearted.
6. Five years later. Same goals. Same January.

**On-screen text:** "5 YEARS LATER" on the cut

**Caption**
"I'm not a consistent person" isn't who you are. It's a story you tell yourself after you miss a day.
Consistent people miss days too. They just don't miss twice.
Comment LOOP if this one hit a bit close.

---

## 3. The "when things calm down" loop

**Belief in the loop:** "Once things calm down, I'll get back into it."

**Script** (~16s)
1. Once things calm down, I'll get back into it.
2. Right now just isn't the right season.
3. I tried fitting it in last month. Work blew up. Should've known.
4. Once things calm down, I'll get back into it.
5. You can't pour from an empty cup. I'll rest first.
6. Five years later. Things never calmed down. I never started.

**On-screen text:** "5 YEARS LATER" on the cut

**Caption**
Things don't calm down. You get better at doing it when they don't.
What would your plan look like on your worst week? Start there.
Comment WORST WEEK and I'll send you how to build one.
