## Transcript

Everyone talks about hooks, but the superhook is what actually gets you views.
There are three types, and you want to say it as the second line of your video.
The first type is results.
This is what you've accomplished.
An example is, I grew 100,000 followers in five weeks.
Here are the three things you need to know about writing hooks.
But if you don't have results yet, then you want to use the effort superhook.
An example is, I spent five weeks testing 700 clod skills.
Here are the only three that actually apply to content creation.
Nobody wants to test 700 clod skills, so they will watch your video and take your word for it.
But the third type is research, where you can borrow and steal credibility.
You can say, Harvard just published a research paper on the latest AI content creation tools.
Even if you don't have credibility, you're borrowing Harvard.
And if you want to see a real example of mine, just look at my on-screen text hook.
