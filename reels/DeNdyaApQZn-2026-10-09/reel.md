## Caption

First of a multi-part series so follow along! Conversations can become so much richer if we just ask the right follow up questions.

## Transcript

If they only give a little detail, and you say tell me more, this will help them feel comfortable to share more, and it will lead to a deeper conversation.
If they only say what happened, and you ask how did that make you feel, this will let them know that you care about them, and they will remember that conversation for years.
If they say something vague, and you ask for an example, this will turn the conversation into a more interesting story, and they will discover something new about themselves.
If they tell you about a choice they made, and you ask what led to that decision, this will uncover their values and motivations, and it will help you understand them better.
