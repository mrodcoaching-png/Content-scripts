## Transcript

If you share a generic story, your audience will ignore you.
But if you share a story and if you share your past with it, your audience will become curious.
But if you share your story, share your past and then share your achievement, they will pay attention.
But if you share your story, share your past, share your achievements and share the steps towards achievement, they will be invested.
But if you share your story, share your past, share your achievements and the steps you took to achieve those things
and then you share all your learnings, they will become your die-hard fans.
